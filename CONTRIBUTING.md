# Contributing to Velumio

Thank you for your interest in contributing to **Velumio**! We are building an open, accessible ecosystem that bridges physical paper books with real-time generative AI companions.

By participating in this project, you agree to abide by our commitment to open, respectful, and collaborative development under the **GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPLv3)**.

---

## How Can You Contribute?

### 1. Reporting Bugs
- Use the [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Andrushon/velumio.ink/issues) tab.
- Clearly describe the unexpected behavior, steps to reproduce, and your environment (OS, tablet model, browser, audio input device).
- Include screenshots or audio logs where applicable.

### 2. Suggesting Enhancements
- Feature requests and ideas (especially around reading accessibility, speech latency, prompt engineering, and offline support) are welcome.
- Open an issue labeled `enhancement` and describe the problem your idea solves.
